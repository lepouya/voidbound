# Deep Dive: Tiles Survive!

## Overview

_Tiles Survive!_ is a 4X strategy survival game by FunPlus. It differentiates
itself from similar games through a unique tile-based exploration and expansion
mechanic during the base-building phase.

## Core Features & Mechanics

1. **Tile-Based Exploration:** The world is grid-based. Players clear fog and
   unlock adjacent tiles to expand their territory, find resources, and create
   space for new buildings. This provides an immediate, physical sense of
   progression.
2. **Settlement Management (Power Plant):** The central structure is the Power
   Plant. Managing electricity connections to other buildings is crucial for
   maintaining base efficiency.
3. **Survivor Management:** Survivors must be assigned to tasks (hunting,
   cooking, logging). The game places a heavy emphasis on monitoring their
   health, morale, and satiety to keep productivity high.
4. **Hero System & Combat:** Features PvE challenges (like Burrow Conquest
   against "Walkers" and "Behemoths") and PvP arenas, utilizing recruited heroes
   with unique traits.
5. **Seasonal Content:** Incorporates seasonal updates that introduce new
   long-term progression loops (e.g., dragon progression in Season 5).

## Gameplay Flow

- **Early Game:** Focuses intensely on the tile-clearing mechanic and base
  infrastructure. Managing survivor needs and connecting buildings to the power
  grid provides an engaging puzzle-like management loop.
- **Mid-Game:** Shifts toward strategic hero development and optimizing resource
  bottlenecks. Joining an active alliance becomes necessary.
- **End-Game:** Similar to other FunPlus titles (like State of Survival), the
  game transitions into a competitive 4X PvP experience with cross-server events
  (e.g., Reservoir Raid). Progression becomes heavily reliant on compounding
  long-term investments and, according to community feedback, monetized bundles.
