# Deep Dive: Last Fortress: Underground

## Overview

_Last Fortress: Underground_ is a unique take on the 4X strategy genre,
presenting its base-building mechanics from a side-scrolling, subterranean
perspective reminiscent of _Fallout Shelter_.

## Core Features & Mechanics

1. **Underground Base-Building:** The settlement is built vertically. Players
   dig out rooms and must strategically place ladders to ensure efficient
   pathing for workers.
2. **Survivor Management:** Assigning survivors to specific rooms (power, water,
   food). Balancing the base's population mood using specific characters (like
   Nate) is an ongoing management task.
3. **Primary Lineup Focus (APCs):** Combat revolves around Armored Personnel
   Carriers (APCs). Strategic depth comes from focusing resources entirely on a
   primary 5-hero lineup before diversifying.
4. **Combat Tactics:** Formation matters. Players must place tanks in the front
   row and damage dealers in the back, often utilizing "tank-leveling"
   strategies (keeping tanks lower level to bait enemy AI).
5. **Season Mechanics:** The game operates on a seasonal model, introducing new
   mechanics, map objectives (like capturing mines), and narrative beats over
   time.

## Gameplay Flow

- **Early Game (Season 0-1):** Focuses on excavating the bunker, establishing a
  stable resource economy, and learning the unique side-scrolling management
  loop.
- **Mid-Game:** The game transitions to the traditional 4X overworld map.
  Players must join strong alliances, capture resource mines, and begin heavy
  investment into their primary combat APC.
- **End-Game:** Dominated by alliance vs. alliance warfare (city sieges) and
  cross-server events. Progression requires meticulous resource hoarding for
  specific event days (e.g., Alliance Trials) to maximize rewards.
