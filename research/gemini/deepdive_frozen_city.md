# Deep Dive: Frozen City

## Overview

_Frozen City_ is a survival and city-building simulation game set in an
ice-covered apocalypse. Unlike traditional 4X games, it focuses heavily on
stage-based progression and intense resource/temperature management rather than
open-world PvP conquest.

## Core Features & Mechanics

1. **The Furnace:** The core mechanic. Players must constantly supply fuel to
   keep survivors warm. The furnace consumes fuel even while the player is
   offline, requiring careful balancing.
2. **Survivor Needs:** Survivors will strike, protest, or die if their needs
   (warmth, food, sleep) are not met. Upgrading dorms and ensuring adequate food
   production is a constant priority.
3. **Stage-Based Progression:** Instead of a single persistent map, the game
   progresses through distinct regions/cities. Players must complete a checklist
   of tasks to advance to the next city, often resetting some progress but
   keeping hero levels.
4. **Hero Management:** Heroes boost production in specific facilities and are
   used in a separate "Adventure" combat mode.
5. **Idle Mechanics:** The game relies heavily on idle farming. Players must
   wait for resources to accumulate offline once their production chains are
   optimized.

## Gameplay Flow

- **Early Game:** Frantic balancing of basic needs. Establishing a steady supply
  of food (Kitchen/Hunter's Cabin) and keeping the furnace fueled are top
  priorities to prevent immediate failure.
- **Mid-Game:** Focus shifts to optimizing production chains and leveling up
  heroes to unlock more work slots in facilities. Progress involves a lot of
  idling and waiting for resources.
- **End-Game:** Managing the long-term grind. Players focus on maximizing hero
  line-ups for the Adventure mode and meticulously balancing late-game resource
  economies before moving to harder regions.
