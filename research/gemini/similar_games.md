# Similar Mobile Strategy/Survival Games

Based on research into games sharing mechanics with **Whiteout Survival**,
**Dark War: Survival**, and **Tiles Survive!**, here is a list of other
prominent titles in this specific 4X (Explore, Expand, Exploit, Exterminate)
mobile strategy subgenre, along with a brief breakdown of each.

## 1. Tiles Survive!

- **Theme:** Post-apocalyptic survival.
- **Mechanics:**
  - Base-building: Focused around a central "Power Plant."
  - Tile-Driven Exploration: The map expands by clearing and unlocking adjacent
    tiles to gather resources or find building space.
  - Survivor Management: Players must assign survivors to roles (hunters, chefs,
    lumberjacks) and manage their health, morale, and satiety.
- **Key Takeaway:** Demonstrates a satisfying early-game loop of literal
  territory expansion (tile by tile) which gives the player immediate, tangible
  progression before the PvP endgame takes over.

## 2. State of Survival

- **Theme:** Post-apocalyptic zombie survival.
- **Mechanics:**
  - Base-building: Players rebuild a settlement, clear debris, and construct
    resource-producing buildings (Food, Wood, Metal, Gas).
  - Combat: Features both RTS-style world map battles and a separate
    tower-defense/RPG minigame (Explorer Trail) using hero abilities.
  - Social: Heavy emphasis on alliances, state vs. state (SvS) warfare, and
    capital control.
- **Key Takeaway:** One of the most successful games in the genre, effectively
  combining the 4X map layer with engaging PvE hero modes. The "end game" is
  essentially infinite power scaling and cross-server warfare.

## 3. Frozen City

- **Theme:** Glacial apocalypse (very similar thematic setup to Whiteout
  Survival).
- **Mechanics:**
  - Focuses more heavily on the survival and town management aspects. You must
    assign workers to tasks, manage their warmth, food, and rest, and balance
    resources carefully.
  - Less emphasis on direct player-vs-player (PvP) map conquest compared to
    Whiteout Survival, leaning closer to a management simulator.
- **Key Takeaway:** Demonstrates that the survival/management loop can be strong
  enough to carry a game without immediately forcing players into aggressive
  PvP.

## 4. Last Fortress: Underground

- **Theme:** Underground bunker survival (resembling Fallout Shelter).
- **Mechanics:**
  - Base-building is vertical (digging down into a bunker) rather than
    horizontal.
  - Players manage survivors, assigning them to different rooms (power, water,
    food).
  - Includes the standard 4X overworld map where alliances fight for territory
    and resources.
- **Key Takeaway:** The unique perspective (side-scrolling bunker) provides a
  fresh take on the standard city-builder, emphasizing the claustrophobic
  survival aspect.

## 5. Puzzles & Survival

- **Theme:** Zombie apocalypse.
- **Mechanics:**
  - Blends the standard 4X base-building and overworld map with Match-3 puzzle
    combat.
  - Hero abilities are charged by matching specific colored gems during combat.
- **Key Takeaway:** Shows how hybridizing the core 4X loop with a completely
  different genre (Match-3) can create a highly engaging daily gameplay loop.

## 6. Age of Origins

- **Theme:** Zombie apocalypse/military strategy.
- **Mechanics:**
  - Traditional city building, troop training, and alliance warfare.
  - Includes a tower defense minigame where players use their troops and heroes
    to defend against waves of zombies.
- **Key Takeaway:** Adding variety through different combat modes (tower
  defense) helps break up the monotony of the standard wait-timer resource loop.

## PC/Console Influences

It's also worth noting the PC/Console games that heavily influence this mobile
genre:

- **Frostpunk / Frostpunk 2:** The direct thematic inspiration for Whiteout
  Survival. Focuses deeply on moral choices, societal management, and harsh
  resource balancing without the P2W mobile elements.
- **Fallout Shelter:** The inspiration for games like Last Fortress, focusing on
  room management and assigning dwellers to specific tasks based on their stats.
