# Deep Dive: Puzzles & Survival

## Overview

_Puzzles & Survival_ successfully merges standard 4X base-building mechanics
with Match-3 puzzle combat in a zombie apocalypse setting, creating a highly
engaging daily gameplay loop.

## Core Features & Mechanics

1. **Match-3 Combat:** Battles against zombies are resolved via matching colored
   tiles. Creating combos charges hero abilities. Elemental matchups
   (rock-paper-scissors mechanics) add tactical depth to the puzzles.
2. **Base Building (Headquarters):** The traditional 4X layer. Players must
   gather resources, upgrade the HQ, and manage research and troop training
   queues.
3. **Hero Factions & Elements:** Collecting a diverse roster of heroes is
   critical. Players must build teams with synergistic elemental strengths to
   overcome difficult PvE campaign stages.
4. **Dual-Queue Building:** A significant mechanic where players are heavily
   incentivized to use premium currency (diamonds) or spend money to unlock a
   second builder queue to maintain pace.
5. **Alliance Shop & Research:** Alliances provide shared research buffs and a
   shop where players can spend alliance coins for crucial progression items.

## Gameplay Flow

- **Early Game:** Extremely fast-paced, guided by the "notepad" quest list.
  Players quickly build their initial base and play through the satisfying
  Match-3 campaign stages.
- **Mid-Game:** Resource requirements spike. Players must meticulously plan HQ
  upgrades by checking prerequisites. The Match-3 stages become difficult,
  requiring specific hero elemental team compositions.
- **End-Game:** The focus shifts to large-scale 4X PvP and alliance warfare. The
  Match-3 combat remains a daily activity but the primary progression gate
  becomes world map dominance, resource gathering, and server politics.
