# Deep Dive: Dark War: Survival

## Overview

_Dark War: Survival_ is a mobile 4X strategy game that blends city-building, RTS
elements, and hero-based combat in a post-apocalyptic zombie setting.

## Core Features & Mechanics

1. **Base Development (Watchtower):** The central hub is the Watchtower, which
   gates progress, hero levels, and feature unlocks. Priority is placed on
   upgrading this as fast as possible.
2. **Resource & Survivor Management:** Balancing food, fuel, and materials while
   assigning rescued survivors to different workstations to maximize
   productivity.
3. **4X Strategy (Radar & World Map):** Once the Radar is unlocked, players
   access the larger world map for competitive gameplay (eXplore, eXpand,
   eXploit, eXterminate).
4. **Hero Investment:** A diverse roster of survivors can be recruited and
   trained. The game allows for resetting hero levels to reclaim resources,
   encouraging strategic swapping.
5. **Competitive Events:** Seasonal and daily events like "Tyrant" boss battles
   or territory wars that require alliance coordination.

## Gameplay Flow

- **Early Game:** Heavily guided by Chapter Tasks and Growth Missions. Players
  focus on establishing the shelter and upgrading the Watchtower to level 8
  quickly to unlock core systems.
- **Mid-Game:** Transition to world map activities. Scouting enemy bases,
  gathering external resources, and participating in alliance rallies against
  "boomers" (boss zombies).
- **End-Game:** Highly competitive and heavily influenced by "pay-to-win"
  dynamics. Active alliance membership is mandatory to defend resources and
  participate in major territory control events like Black Gold Battlefield.
