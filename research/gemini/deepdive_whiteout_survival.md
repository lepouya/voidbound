# Deep Dive: Whiteout Survival

## Overview

_Whiteout Survival_ is a mobile real-time strategy and city-builder set in a
glacial apocalypse. Players must balance city management, resource gathering,
and competitive expansion.

## Core Features & Mechanics

1. **The Furnace (Central Hub):** The heart of the city that provides heat.
   Players must constantly supply it with coal. The Furnace level dictates the
   level cap of all other buildings.
2. **Survivor Management:** Survivors arrive at the city and must be assigned to
   jobs (hunter, cook, woodcutter). They have basic needs like health and warmth
   that must be managed to maintain productivity.
3. **Resource System:** Four primary resources: Meat, Wood, Coal, Iron. A common
   production ratio strategy is 4:4:2:1.
4. **Hero System:** Players recruit heroes via a gacha system to lead troops in
   PvE exploration and PvP combat.
5. **Alliance Dynamics:** Mandatory for mid/late-game progression. Alliances
   offer speed-up helps, territory buffs, and access to large-scale events.

## Gameplay Flow

- **Early Game (Days 1-20):** Focuses heavily on "rushing" the Furnace level to
  unlock core features like Intel, Exploration, and Pets. The loop involves
  micro-managing survivors' needs and continuous base building.
- **Mid-Game:** Resource scarcity hits. Players must venture onto the world map
  to gather resources, exposing themselves to PvP. Joining an active alliance
  becomes critical for survival.
- **End-Game:** The city-building aspect fades into the background. Gameplay
  revolves around daily routines, event participation (like Crazy Joe, Bear
  Hunts, Canyon Clash), and server-vs-server (SvS) warfare. The experience
  becomes highly repetitive and reliant on alliance coordination and "Whale"
  players.
