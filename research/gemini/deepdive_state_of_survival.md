# Deep Dive: State of Survival

## Overview

_State of Survival_ is one of the most successful mobile strategy MMORPGs,
combining base building, resource management, and a distinct hero-led PvE mode
within a zombie apocalypse setting.

## Core Features & Mechanics

1. **Base Building (Headquarters):** The traditional settlement management loop.
   Players upgrade the HQ to unlock higher-tier resource nodes (Farms,
   Lumberyards) and troop training facilities.
2. **Explorer Trail (PvE Mini-game):** A distinct single-player, real-time
   combat mode where players control a squad of heroes using their unique
   abilities to clear stages and progress a narrative. This breaks up the
   monotony of base building.
3. **Hero System:** A robust gacha system for recruiting and upgrading heroes,
   who are essential for both the Explorer Trail and leading armies on the world
   map.
4. **World Map:** The standard 4X overworld where players gather resources,
   fight wandering infected, and engage in PvP against other settlements.

## Gameplay Flow

- **Early Game:** Fast-paced progression guided strictly by an in-game task
  list. Players focus on establishing resource pipelines and clearing initial
  PvE stages.
- **Mid-Game:** Upgrade timers increase significantly. The daily routine shifts
  to clearing intel missions, gathering resources continuously, and joining an
  alliance for protection and group rallies (Infected Fiends).
- **End-Game:** The game becomes a large-scale political and military simulator.
  The focus is almost entirely on "State vs. State" (SvS) cross-server warfare,
  maximizing late-game power systems (like Plasma levels and Strategic
  Archives), and maintaining alliance dominance.
