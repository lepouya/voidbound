# Deep Dive: Age of Origins

## Overview

_Age of Origins_ (formerly _Age of Z Origins_) is a 4X mobile strategy game that
distinguishes itself by integrating tower defense mini-games directly into its
zombie apocalypse city-building loop.

## Core Features & Mechanics

1. **Tower Defense:** A unique PvE mode located at the city entrance. Players
   place and upgrade various towers (Machine Gun, Rocket, EMP, Laser) to stop
   waves of zombies, breaking up the standard 4X wait timers.
2. **City-Building & Economy:** Upgrading the City Hall is the primary goal, but
   the game heavily emphasizes establishing a strong underlying economy
   (production buildings) before rushing HQ levels.
3. **Army Management:** Features multiple tiers of soldiers, deployable Titans
   (massive single units), and warplanes.
4. **Officer Recruitment:** Players recruit and assign officers to lead troops.
   The strategy involves focusing resources heavily on high-tier ("purple")
   officers and ignoring lower tiers.
5. **Capital Wars:** The pinnacle of the game's social layer, where massive
   alliances compete for server-wide control.

## Gameplay Flow

- **Early Game:** Players rebuild their ruined city, complete daily missions,
  and engage heavily in the fun, active tower defense mode. Joining an active
  alliance immediately is the most critical strategic move.
- **Mid-Game:** Focus shifts to continuous research (Academy) and troop
  training. Players must manage their "fuel" (stamina) efficiently, constantly
  attacking zombies on the world map to maintain a resource surplus.
- **End-Game:** Dominated by server-wide events (Capital Wars, Polar Invasion).
  Individual base building slows to a crawl, and the game becomes a strategic
  military simulator reliant on alliance reinforcements and coordinated attacks.
