# Tiles Survive! — evidence notes

## Product frame

FunPlus describes Tiles Survive! as a wilderness shelter game in which the
player expands into tiles/biomes, gathers resources, builds and upgrades,
connects electricity, assigns survivors to jobs, attends to health and morale,
travels across maps for collectibles, recruits heroes, and forms alliances
[S10]. The official hub also shows an extensive live-operations layer with
alliance conquest, rallies, gear, events, cosmetics, and season competition
[S08].

## Core loop

1. Reveal/claim tiles to gain space, access resources, and push into a new
   biome.
2. Construct and upgrade a shelter production network; add electricity to
   improve efficiency.
3. Assign survivors while keeping health and morale productive.
4. Explore additional maps, collect special resources/collectibles, and return
   them to the base.
5. Progress through heroes, alliance coordination, and event/season systems.

The tile-expansion framing is the clearest of the three references for a solo
player: every outward move visibly changes what the player can reach.
Electricity and worker needs imply a production-puzzle layer instead of only an
HQ-gate layer. We still need hands-on validation of how much of this remains
meaningful past the opening.

## Supporting engagement systems

- Biome and multi-map exploration; collectibles/decorations [S10].
- Survivor job assignment, health/morale, production buildings, and power
  connections [S10].
- Heroes and alliances [S10].
- Officially documented seasonal conquest, rallies, gear reforging, chat, event
  checklist, cosmetics, and an arena/goldmine layer [S08].

## Community feedback to preserve

The current Google Play reviews prominently include reports that the actual
experience differs from ads and that hero-fragment acquisition lags hero
releases [S10]. These are individual verified reviews, not quantitative
findings. They support two design checks for Voidbound: its first playable
minutes should be an honest sample of the campaign, and new options must be
earnable through a clear local path rather than only introduced as distant
collection pressure.

## Applicability to Voidbound

**Borrow:** outward, geography-led progression; visible resource access;
production dependencies that are intelligible in-world; exploratory side maps;
and a shelter that tells a story through its layout.

**Adapt:** Voidbound already has a radial star system, asteroid grades,
mothership modules, crew assignment, mining vessels, and future expeditions. Use
those as its “tiles”: each sector/route should answer a new need or choice, not
simply add territory.

**Avoid in Phase 3:** treating worker health/morale as another invisible bar,
feature-stack overload, and live-event/season compulsion.
